Pancakes🥞

Description

pancake is a thin, flat cake made from a batter of flour, milk, and eggs, cooked on both sides in a frying pan or on a griddle.

Ingredients

  • Flour and Baking powder
  • Sugar and salt
  • Milk and Eggs
  • Butter or Oil

Steps

  1. Whisk the dry ingredients together.
  2. Mix the wet ingredients together.
  3. Combine the wet and dry ingredients.
  4. Heat a greased skillet.
  5. Pour the batter and cook until bubbly.
  6. Flip and cook until golden brown.
