Burger🍔

Description

A burger is a popular sandwich made with a cooked ground meat patty placed inside a sliced bread roll or bun.

Ingredients

  • Patties
  • Buns
  • Toppings
  • Cheese
  • Sauces

Steps

  1. Toast the buns.
  2. Cook the patties.
  3. Melt the cheese on the patties.
  4. Spread the sauces on the buns.
  5. Layer the toppings and assemble the burger
